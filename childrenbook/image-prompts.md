# Image Generation Prompts for "Goldie and Draghino"

Use these prompts in ChatGPT (DALL-E), Midjourney, or any AI image generator.
Save each image as `page-01.png`, `page-02.png`, etc. in the `childrenbook/images/` folder.

**Recommended settings:**
- Square format (1024x1024 or 1:1 aspect ratio)
- Children's book illustration style
- Consistent style across all images

**Base style prefix (add to every prompt):**
> Children's picture book illustration, soft watercolor style, warm and whimsical, for ages 6-10.

## Character Descriptions (reference for all prompts)

**Goldie:** A cute, chubby stuffed-animal-style blue dragon with sparkly blue fur/scales, a shiny golden scaled belly, very large amber/gold eyes (like Beanie Boo toys — oversized and glossy), small black wings, a yellow nose marking, and a short tail. Compact and round body.

**Draghino:** A cute, chubby stuffed-animal-style dragon covered in pastel rainbow colors — mostly pink with purple, yellow, green, and orange spots/patches. Very large teal/turquoise eyes (Beanie Boo style), three small blue horns on top of head, small dark wings with blue tips, blue-tipped toes, a light pink/white snout. Round and pudgy body.
